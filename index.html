How it works

  1. 1Drop as many audio files as you like: MP3, WAV, OGG and more. Nothing leaves your device.
  2. 2Drag tracks to set the order, and drag the handles on each waveform to trim in and out.
  3. 3Add a transition between any two tracks: a crossfade, or a fade-out into fade-in.
  4. 4Preview the whole sequence, then hit Export Audio to render and download an MP3 right in your browser.
Everything stays on your device — no uploads, no clouds.

About mix&match

mix&match is a minimal, cozy little audio editor that lives entirely in your browser. Combine, trim, and transition your favourite recordings into one seamless mix, with the Web Audio API doing all the heavy lifting.

No accounts. No uploads. No clouds. Your sound never leaves your machine.
